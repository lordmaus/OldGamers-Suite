# Privacy Policy — Badge Overlay

_Last updated: 2026-05-29_

## Summary

Badge Overlay does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no telemetry, no analytics, and no network communication.

## What the app stores

The app saves your preferences (badge colors, detector rules, hotkeys, window position, theme, and accessibility settings) **locally** on your computer under:

```
%AppData%\BadgeOverlay\
```

These files never leave your machine. You can delete them at any time to reset the app.

## What the app does NOT do

- No data is sent to any server or third party.
- No usage statistics, crash reports, or identifiers are collected or transmitted.
- No advertising or tracking SDKs are included.

## Screen content & accessibility

Badge Overlay reads on-screen UI element information through the Windows UI Automation / MSAA APIs to position badges, and can speak element labels via the local Windows text-to-speech engine (System.Speech) when Screen Reader Mode is enabled. All of this processing happens **on-device**. No screen contents, audio, or detected text are recorded or transmitted.

## Your control

Because all data is local, you are in full control. Uninstalling the app and deleting the `%AppData%\BadgeOverlay\` folder removes everything.
